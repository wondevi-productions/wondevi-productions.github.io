Annual budget calculator: your whole year in one number

Rent is monthly, groceries are weekly, rego is yearly, the power bill is quarterly - so it's hard to see what a year really costs. Put each bill in with how often you pay it, and this turns everything into one yearly total and shows what's left over.

How it works

  • Each amount is turned into a yearly figure: weekly x 52, fortnightly x 26, monthly x 12, quarterly x 4, yearly x 1.
  • Left over per year = yearly income minus yearly spending. The monthly figure is that divided by 12.
  • 52 weeks and 26 fortnights are used as a standard year; a year with an extra pay day will differ slightly.

Example with the numbers filled in: $2,400 take-home pay a fortnight is $62,400 a year. The eight bills add up to $36,570 a year, which leaves $25,830 - about $2,152.50 a month on average.
