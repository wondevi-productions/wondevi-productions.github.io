What a valid tax invoice must show (ATO)

If you are registered for GST, a customer can ask you for a tax invoice for any sale over $82.50 including GST, and you have to give one within 28 days. For sales under $1,000 it must show:

  • that it is a tax invoice
  • your identity (business name) and your ABN
  • the date it was issued
  • a brief description of what you sold, including the quantity where it applies
  • the GST amount, or a statement such as "Total price includes GST"
  • the extent each item is taxable

For sales of $1,000 or more it must also show the buyer's identity or ABN. Not registered for GST? Untick the box above: the heading changes to "Invoice" and no GST is added, because you can't charge GST or issue a tax invoice. Check the current rules on the ATO website.

Tradie invoicing habits that get you paid faster

  • Invoice on the day. Send it from the job before you drive off. Every day you wait is a day added to when you get paid.
  • Itemise labour, parts and call-out. Customers argue with one big number, not with clear lines. List your call-out or travel charge as its own line, and price parts with a proper markup.
  • Put payment details on the invoice. BSB, account and PayID right there removes the "how do I pay you?" delay.
  • Short terms. 7 days is common for residential customers. Follow up the day after it is due.
  • Quote first, invoice to the quote. If the invoice matches what they agreed to, there is nothing to dispute. Use the free job quote calculator to build the number.
