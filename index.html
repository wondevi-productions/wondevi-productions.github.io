How the calculator works

Most people set their rate by looking at what others charge. The trouble is your costs and your billable hours are not the same as theirs. This calculator uses the only formula that tells you whether a rate actually pays:

Break-even rate = (annual overheads + your wage) / billable hours per year

Billable hours are weeks worked x hours per week x utilisation. Utilisation is the part people get wrong: driving between jobs, parts runs, quoting, invoicing and call-backs don't get billed. For mobile mechanics and tradies it's usually 55-70%. At 46 weeks x 45 hours x 65%, that's about 1,345 billable hours, far fewer than the 2,070 you're on the tools or on the road.
