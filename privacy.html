Privacy Policy

Wondevi Productions (WondeviDesigns), Queensland, Australia. Last updated 19 November 2026 (visit counting added).

This website

This website (wondevi-productions.github.io) is a static site. We do not use cookies, advertising trackers, sign-ups or contact forms, and we do not collect personal information through it. We count page visits and clicks on our store links with GoatCounter (no cookies, no personal data). The free calculators on these pages run only in your browser; the numbers you type are not sent to us or stored by us.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses to run and secure its service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Buying our products

Our digital products are sold through third-party stores: Payhip, Gumroad and Etsy. When you buy, the store processes your payment and order details under its own privacy policy. We receive the order information the store shares with sellers (for example your name, email address and what you bought) and use it only to deliver the product and provide support. We do not sell or rent this information.

Our Pinterest integration

We use Pinterest's official API to publish Pins about our own products to our own Pinterest business account (WondeviDesigns). The integration:

  • only acts on our own Pinterest account, with our permission;
  • reads our own boards so it can choose where a Pin goes, and creates Pins that link to our own product pages;
  • does not access, collect or store information about other Pinterest users;
  • keeps its access token privately on our own computer and does not share it.

Contact

For privacy questions or to ask us to delete information we hold about you, open an issue at github.com/wondevi-productions/wondevi-productions.github.io/issues (do not include personal details in a public issue; we will reply with a private way to continue), or contact us through the store you bought from.
