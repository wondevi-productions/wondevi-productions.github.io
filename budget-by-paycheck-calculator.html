Budget by paycheck: which bills fall before your next pay?

Paid weekly or fortnightly but your bills come monthly? Some pays carry the rent and the car loan, others carry almost nothing. Put in your next pay date and your bills to see exactly what this pay has to cover, and what is left to spend.

How it works

  • This pay covers every bill due from your pay date up to the day before your next pay.
  • Monthly bills land on the same day each month; a bill due on the 29th, 30th or 31st falls on the last day of shorter months.
  • Left to spend = take-home pay minus savings minus the bills due this pay.

Example with the numbers filled in: paid fortnightly from 4 December 2026, the phone (15th), electricity (22nd) and insurance (28th) fall in the first pay, while rent (1st) and the car loan (10th) land in later pays. That is why one pay can feel rich and the next one short.
