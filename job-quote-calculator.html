Job quote calculator

Price one job from hours, travel and parts. Enter your own rates. The result updates as you type. Nothing is uploaded.

Labour-hours x rate
Travel-call-out + km
Parts on the quote-cost plus markup
Subtotal ex GST-includes consumables
GST-10% when ticked
Customer total-inc GST

What each line means

  • Labour is hours on the tools times your charge-out rate. Get that rate from the hourly rate calculator. Use the ex-GST rate.
  • Travel is a call-out fee plus kilometres times your per-km rate. The hourly calculator also shows van cost per km, which is the floor for that rate. See travel and call-out fees.
  • Parts is what you paid, then your markup. A 50% markup on $80 of parts is $120 on the quote. See the parts markup guide.
  • GST is 10% of the ex-GST subtotal, added only if you tick the box. Tick it when you are registered for GST and the job is taxable.

Worked example with the numbers already filled in: 2 hours at $110 = $220 labour, $0 call-out + 20 km at $1.50 = $30 travel, parts $80 plus 50% = $120, consumables $10. Subtotal $380. GST $38. Customer total $418.

A quoting aid only. It does not set your prices, and it is not tax advice. Confirm GST with your accountant.
